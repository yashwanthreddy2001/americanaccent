American sounds to watch

  • R: curl your tongue back, do not touch the roof of your mouth.
  • Flap T: "water" sounds like "wader", "better" like "bedder".
  • The "ae" vowel: open your mouth wide in "cat", "map", "happy".
  • TH: tongue between teeth for "think" and "this".
